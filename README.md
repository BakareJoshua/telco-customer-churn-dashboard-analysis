# Telco Customer Churn Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a Telco Customer Churn dataset to understand why customers are leaving the business and which customer groups are most at risk.

The dashboard helps stakeholders identify key churn patterns across contract type, payment method, internet service, tenure group, tech support, online security, and monthly charges.

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel
- CSV Dataset

## Dataset

The dataset contains 7,043 customer records and 21 columns. Each row represents one customer.

The dataset includes customer information such as:

- Customer status
- Contract type
- Payment method
- Internet service
- Tech support
- Online security
- Tenure
- Monthly charges
- Churn status

## Key Metrics

- Total Customers: 7,043
- Churned Customers: 1,869
- Retained Customers: 5,174
- Churn Rate: 26.54%
- Average Monthly Charge of Churned Customers: 74.44

## Key Insights

- The overall churn rate is 26.54%, meaning about 1 in 4 customers left the business.
- Month-to-month contract customers had the highest churn count.
- Customers within 0–12 months had the highest churn among tenure groups.
- Electronic check users had the highest churn among payment methods.
- Fiber optic customers had the highest churn among internet service users.
- Customers without tech support and online security were more likely to leave.
- Churned customers had higher average monthly charges and shorter average tenure.

## Recommendations

- Create retention offers for month-to-month customers.
- Improve customer support during the first 12 months.
- Review pricing for customers with high monthly charges.
- Investigate fiber optic customer experience.
- Promote tech support and online security services.
- Encourage more stable payment methods.
- Track churn monthly to measure improvement.
